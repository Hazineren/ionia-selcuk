SELÇUK · 37°57′N 27°22′E

ONE DISTRICT, MANY CHAPTERS

Read the landscape, not just the ruins.

Selçuk is a working town of farmers, makers and families who live beside a World Heritage property. The coastline that once reached Ephesus has moved west, and the town has moved with it. Visit the monuments, then stay for the market, the olive groves and the village table: that is where tourism supports local life.

IN THE HILLS · ŞİRİNCE

A village worth the night.

A short drive above Selçuk, Şirince’s stone houses, churches and olive terraces earned it a place among UN Tourism’s Best Tourism Villages in 2023. Stay overnight, eat in a family kitchen and buy directly from the people who make the wine, soap and preserves.

Selçuk Efes Camel Wrestling Festival

A long-running winter tradition of the Aegean countryside, with a parade through town the day before and wrestling at the arena in Pamucak, Selçuk. Organised locally by Selçuk Municipality and partners.
